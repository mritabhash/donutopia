Privacy

Updated 19 October 2026.

What stays in your browser

Game progress, scores, streaks and preferences are saved in local browser storage. Clearing site data removes these copies. Donutopia does not currently offer account syncing or a global leaderboard.

Answer judging

Your answer and its prompt are sent to our Cloudflare Worker for AI judging. The Worker caches the canonical answer and judgment for seven days. It does not save submitted scores or player IDs. Avoid including personal information in your answers.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Cloudflare hosts the judging service and processes network information needed to serve requests. The Worker uses your IP address for short-term request limiting, but does not write it to the judgment cache.

Advertising and payments

The game does not display advertisements or collect payments. No advertising or analytics scripts are loaded by this version.

Contact

For questions, use the project issue page. Issues are public, so do not post private information there.
