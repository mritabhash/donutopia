DONUTOPIA

Privacy

effective September 23, 2026

Donutopia is a daily game at krillion.io, run by one person. The short version: you can play without an account, a name, or an email. The game tells players apart with a random id your own browser invents, which says nothing about who you are. Card numbers never touch our servers. We don’t sell or rent anything we hold. Advertising on the site is handled by Mediavine — the advertising section below explains exactly what that involves.

The long version follows.

what stays on your device

Guest progress, games in progress, device preferences (like sound and colorblind mode), your selected character, the random player id, and purchase unlock tokens live in your browser’s local storage. Clearing site data erases these local copies. When you use a Google account for progress sync, completed games also have an account copy on our server. Purchases can be restored with your unlock link or linked Google account.

what reaches our server

Every answer you type is sent to our server to be checked — that’s how scoring works. Synced completed games keep their recorded answers in your private account history. Separately, website guest daily answers are checked and discarded, not stored. One exception: answers to the free practice dive are recorded (with the random player id) so we can tune how answers are scored.

When you finish a dive, we store the game, your score, and the random player id. That’s what powers the “better than X% of divers” stat — it’s a scoreboard of anonymous ids, not people.

If you use the suggestion box, we store the text you wrote and the random player id it came from. Don’t put personal details in there.

If you rate a dive on the results screen, we store the rating, any note you add, your score for that dive, and the random player id. Same rule: no personal details in the note.

friends

Friends is optional. When you join, we store your chosen username, invite link, friendships, and blocked players. Anyone you share your invite link with can add you as a friend. Replace the link at any time to stop the old one working. You can remove or block friends from the Friends page.

Your leaderboard is visible only to you and includes only your friends. Friends see your username and daily score after finishing their own daily. “Share my answers with friends” starts on, letting friends see that day’s answers after finishing their own daily. Sharing was also enabled once for existing accounts when this feature launched. This account setting applies to daily games until you turn it off. Turning it off stops sharing answers; your score stays visible. You can always see your own answers. Your email address and full game history stay private. Scores come from synced completed games, including history you choose to import.

Discord Activity

This section applies only to the Donutopia Discord Activity, played inside Discord. Its saved progress and sharing settings are separate from the browser game at krillion.io.

In the Activity, we use your Discord user ID and display name to save your daily dive, recorded answers, score, history, and streak. Your in-progress round and its deadline are saved too.

Joining channel standings is optional. We store the channel ID and your sharing preference. Joined players in that channel can see your name; they can see your daily score after finishing their own dive. Your answers stay private unless you turn on answer sharing for that channel.

Daily recaps are a separate, optional setting for each channel. If enabled, your Discord name, score, and seven-round dive graph can appear in a message visible to everyone in that channel after the daily reset. Recaps contain no answers and do not ping players. They start with the day you opt in and are posted when someone next launches the Activity. We store an aggregate record of days with participating divers for the group streak, and a delivery record to prevent repeated recap messages. Turning recaps off stops future sharing; messages already posted remain in Discord.

Leaving standings removes your membership and sharing preferences immediately. It keeps your personal history. Sharing a result through Discord is your choice: Copy result copies the daily number, score, and emoji row, with no name or answers. You choose where to paste it.

We request Discord's identify permission, not your email, messages, contacts, or server list. Access tokens are held in the Activity's memory and used to verify your identity; we do not save them in our database or browser storage. The Activity does not load website advertising or Google Analytics.

Discord provides identity and Activity hosting/proxy services. Vercel hosts Donutopia and Neon stores its saved game data. We keep your history until you request deletion, and we do not sell it. For a copy, correction, or deletion of your Discord data, contact support@krillion.io with your Discord user ID. We will verify the request before acting on it.

cookies

We set exactly one cookie of our own, krillion_session, and only after you buy or restore a purchase. It proves to our server which packs this device owns, can’t be read by scripts, and expires after a year. There are no tracking cookies of ours, ever. Google Analytics (below) sets its own cookies to measure visits, and when ads are served, Mediavine and its partners set advertising cookies — the advertising section covers those in full.

analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages get visited, which games get finished, and what breaks — things like “a game was completed” or “an unlock was restored,” plus the browser and device type and the approximate location Google derives from your IP address. Google’s handling of this data is covered by the Google privacy policy, and you can opt out with the GA opt-out add-on or any content blocker — the game runs exactly the same with analytics blocked.

payments

Purchases happen on a checkout page hosted by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch krillion.io. What we receive back is a payment reference confirming the checkout paid; we keep that reference and the product it unlocked so your purchase can be restored later. Stripe keeps your email for the receipt — their side is covered by the Stripe privacy policy. If you write in about a lost unlock, we look your purchase up in Stripe by the email you write from.

google sign-in

Google sign-in is optional and available to everyone. Firebase Authentication provides your account id, name, and email. We use your identity to restore purchases and, when you connect My Donutopia, save completed daily, archive, and pack games to your account. Synced history includes scores, tiers, prompts, and recorded answers so you can view your results on another device. Streaks and earned characters are derived from that history. We store progress in our Neon database under your Firebase account id; it is private to your account.

We ask before importing existing guest history. Overlapping games keep the highest score and count once. Games in progress and device settings do not sync. Signing out stops syncing and returns to separate guest history; account history remains available when you sign in again. We keep your saved history until you ask us to delete it by contacting support@krillion.io from your account email. We never send marketing email.

abuse protection

IP addresses are used in the moment for rate limiting — stopping scripted abuse, not tracking players. Where a limit needs memory, the IP is stored only as a salted one-way hash that can’t be turned back into an address. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard short-lived server logs.

who touches the data

These services process data on our behalf, each holding only what its job needs: Vercel (hosting), Neon (database), Google (analytics and optional sign-in), Stripe (payments), and Mediavine and its partners (advertising, detailed below). Processing happens in the United States. Nobody else gets anything.

your data, your call

Everything on your device you can erase yourself by clearing the site’s data in your browser. For anything on our side — seeing what we hold, correcting it, or deleting it — email support@krillion.io and we’ll sort it out. For purchase records, write from the email you bought with; note that deleting a purchase record means it can no longer be restored. Scores are stored under the random id, not your name, so include the id if you want those gone too.

children

Donutopia isn’t directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we’ll delete it.

changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. Material changes get a note on the site, not a silent edit.
