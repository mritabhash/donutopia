FAQ

How does the game work?

Answer seven prompts, with 25 seconds per prompt. Valid answers earn points based on their estimated rarity.

Who judges the answers?

Cloudflare Workers AI checks whether your answer fits the prompt and estimates its rarity. There is no fixed list of accepted answers. The question bank currently contains 351 prompts across 12 categories.

Can the judge get it wrong?

Yes. Rarity is an estimate, not a measurement of how often players submit an answer. Spelling variants may be recognised. If judging is unavailable, the game asks you to retry.

When does the daily batch reset?

At midnight in New York. Unlimited mode and recipe boxes offer more batches.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser. Clearing site data removes that local progress. Scores do not sync between devices.
